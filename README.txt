Harry Potter 25th Anniversary fan site
=========================================
To view it: double-click index.html (it opens in your web browser).

Files
  index.html      the main page
  secret.html     the secret Room of Requirement (the games)
  credits.html    photo credits
  css/style.css   colours + layout of the main page (change the colours at the top!)
  css/secret.css  extra styles for the secret room
  js/main.js      stars, sparkles, Sorting Hat, and the easter egg
  js/snake.js     Nagini's Hunt (settings like speed are at the top of the file)
  js/snitch.js    Catch the Snitch
  img/            the photos

Have fun changing things! If something breaks, undo your last change.
